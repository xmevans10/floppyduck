# park Theme Prompt

## hero
```
16-bit pixel art final boss hero background for a side-scrolling arcade flying game, Park theme / The Duck's Domain, wide 3:2 composition.
Grand formal park at dramatic golden afternoon light with a clear blue sky and warm sun rays. Lower third has a regal stone footbridge over a reflective pond, a winding path leading toward a small ornate gazebo/bandstand like a boss arena landmark, trimmed hedges, old-fashioned lamp posts, side-edge benches, and broad leafy trees framing the far left/right edges.
Make it feel like The Duck's private domain without showing The Duck: triumphant, royal, slightly intense, final-stage energy, but still a bright outdoor park rather than a dark castle. Use symmetry, depth, golden highlights, a crown-like gazebo roof silhouette, and gold finials for boss-level identity.
Keep the central flight corridor very clear from left to right. Visual interest should come from large readable shapes, bridge arch, pond reflection, distant gazebo, diagonal path, and tree framing, not dense micro-detail. Put most flowers, stones, lamps, benches, and pond texture in the lower quarter and side edges.
More interesting than a plain park, but still 25-33% less noisy than a dense scenic painting. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, clean arcade readability, saturated but controlled greens, warm gold sunlight, sky blues, soft clouds.
No characters, no duck, no animals, no UI, no text, no pipes, no large objects blocking center, no dense flower carpets, no kite strings or bunting across the flight corridor, no heavy black silhouettes, no painterly/vector/3D style.
```
